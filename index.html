§ 01 — IDEA ONE

Long Passes.

CINEMATICRIGHTS · MEDIUM3 ENVIRONMENTS

Kid plays in their hometown. Kid hits a long pass. The ball flies along the wall LEDs, the length of Dribble World. At the far end, the same player — now an adult, in their team's colours — receives it on their chest and plays on. Then plays it back.

Reference: curved LED installation showing a multi-screen soccer environment. Walls + floor LED, no ceiling LED.

Where it started → who they became. Connected by one ball.

You walk into Dribble World. The walls show cities from around the world — a Buenos Aires potrero, a Bondy concrete cage, a Bambali red-earth pitch, a Rio favela court, a tournament in a small French town. Kids are playing.

One kid is highlighted. They drag the ball through two friends with a flick most ten-year-olds couldn't dream of. They look up. They hit a long pass.

The ball traces in slow motion across the wall LEDs — the full length of Dribble World. Spatial audio carries the whoosh. If you walk with it, you follow it. At the far end, the same player as an adult — in their club colours — reads the pass, brings it down on their chest, and plays on. Then plays it back. Cycle continues.

The same kid. The same pass. Eighteen years apart. The wall is the canvas, the visitor is the witness in the middle.

Why it works

The most cinematic moment in Dribble World. A single coherent narrative beat, easy to grasp, emotionally landed. Earns the wall-length scale — there's a reason it's wide.

Rights story

If we get player rights, we use real legends + their archival childhood photos. If we don't, we use composite kids + non-named pro-style adult footage. Either way, the idea reads.

Women's World Cup hook

Half the kid → adult pairings should be women players. Rising stars, not just the obvious names. Action item: Jorge to research.

Tech notes

  • Wall LED — high-res, edge-to-edge ball trajectory
  • Floor LED — soft pulse at takeoff and chest-trap
  • No ceiling LED needed for this one
  • Object-based spatial audio — pass whoosh travels with the ball

Three environments — first picks

  • Buenos Aires potrero (kid #1) → adult elsewhere
  • Rio favela court (kid #2) → adult elsewhere
  • French town tournament (kid #3) → adult elsewhere

§ 02 — IDEA TWO

Walk Along.

INTERACTIVESIMPLE TECHSPONSORSHIP-READY

Korean-experience reference. Characters on the wall LED follow you as you walk. Stand still and things happen. At the start of Dribble World, they're kids. By the end, they're the famous players — and you've walked their journey through clubs, cities and continents.

Reference: curved LED corridor where the wall content reacts to the visitor's position. Korean-style interactive experiences resolved with simple camera tracking.

A character picks you. Walks with you. Grows up alongside you.

The wall LED detects you with a simple camera. A character on the screen attaches to you — they walk when you walk, stop when you stop. Stand still and they do tricks, juggle, shoot at the wall. Move on and they follow.

At the start, your character is a kid in their hometown. As you progress through Dribble World, the wall tells their story: the streets they grew up on, the youth team that scouted them, the small club, the move abroad, the league they broke into, the team they play for now.

By the time you reach the end, your character is an adult superstar in full kit, in their stadium. You walked their journey. They walked it with you.

Multiple visitors = multiple characters at once on the wall — each one assigned, each one tracking its person. Saw this work in Korea — simple tech, strong result, a really fun thing to do.

Why it works

Yeon: "It's easy. It's easy for people to understand." Visitors instantly get it — character follows me, I walk, story unfolds. Zero learning curve.

The avatar hook

Could integrate the FIFA avatars the client is building — your avatar plays alongside the legend's. Could also use the ball from the other games as your character's ball. Slam-dunk cross-activation moment.

Sponsorship spinoff

Yeon flagged a "kids zone" version as a low-lift sponsorship opportunity — smaller LED wall, kids pick a character, mirror them, kick it back. "20 minutes of jumping jacks for a three-year-old." A great rights/sponsorship deal.

Women's World Cup hook

Same as Long Passes — half the character roster should be women players. Some young rising stars whose journey is JUST being written.

Tech notes

  • Wall LED — main canvas
  • Single camera per zone — simple computer vision tracks people, assigns characters
  • Floor LED can show the character's path / trail
  • Audio shifts per chapter — childhood music → stadium roar
